About C.V.P

One portal
Every institution.

Verifying a single certificate used to mean a trip to campus, a letter to a registrar, or weeks of silence. CVP replaces all of that with one platform that connects you directly to issuing institutions — schools, universities, and examination bodies like WAEC and NECO — so you get a real, confirmed answer in days, not weeks.

Whether you're confirming one certificate or checking results for an entire admission or hiring cycle, you do it from the same dashboard, every time.
